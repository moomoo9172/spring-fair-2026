About the Event

A Fair for the Whole Community

The Spring Fair is Roberto Clemente Elementary School's biggest annual celebration — a day that brings together students, families, staff, and the wider Pittsburgh community.

All proceeds from the Spring Fair go directly toward funding new classroom resources, playground equipment, and student enrichment programmes for the 2026–2027 school year.

🎓

Student Showcases

Art displays, science projects, and performances by our talented students throughout the day.

🍔

Food & Refreshments

Local food vendors, baked goods stall, lemonade stand, and a dedicated kids' snack zone.

🎪

Games & Prizes

Classic fairground games, raffle draws, and plenty of prizes for all ages.

🌱

Community Stalls

Local businesses and community organisations are welcome to set up stalls.

Day Schedule

What's Happening & When

A full day of activities from morning to evening — there's something for everyone.

🌅
8:00 AM – 9:00 AM
Doors Open & Setup
Early birds welcome! Volunteer teams set up booths and the grounds open for the first guests.
🎤
9:00 AM – 9:30 AM
Opening Ceremony
Welcome remarks from the Principal, student performances, and the official ribbon-cutting to kick off the fair.
🎠
9:30 AM – 12:00 PM
Morning Activities
All booths open — games, food stalls, art showcase, science displays, and the kids' activity zone in full swing.
🍕
12:00 PM – 1:00 PM
Lunch Break
Grab a bite from our food vendors! Live acoustic music playing in the courtyard during the lunch hour.
🎭
1:00 PM – 3:00 PM
Afternoon Performances
Student choir, drama club showcase, and a special guest performance on the main stage.
🏆
3:00 PM – 4:00 PM
Raffle Draw & Closing
Grand raffle prize announcements, thank-you remarks, and a final farewell from our students. Must be present to win!
